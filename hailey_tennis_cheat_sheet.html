🎾 Hailey's Tennis Day Cheat Sheet 🎾

Move Your Feet · Have Fun · Be Proud
Serve
TOSS → REACH → SNAP
  • Steady toss out in front
  • Reach up tall and loose
  • Hit up and snap over
  • Follow through across your body
💭 “Up and over!”
Forehand
TURN → DRIVE → FINISH
  • Turn your shoulders early
  • Watch the ball all the way
  • Stay loose and relaxed
  • Hit through the ball to your target
💭 “Relax, hit through!”
Backhand
TURN → STEP → DRIVE
  • Turn early, hands ready
  • Step into the ball
  • Swing low to high
  • Finish high and balanced
💭 “Turn and through!”
Volley
READY → PUNCH → FORWARD
  • Racket up and ready
  • Short, firm punch — don't swing big
  • Catch the ball in front
  • Move forward through it
💭 “Block and go!”
